About RPS Arena

RPS Arena is a fun and interactive Rock, Paper, Scissors game created as part of our group hackathon project.

Our goal was to create a simple, accessible and enjoyable game where players can compete against the computer. They choose a Best of 5 or Best of 9 match, and whoever reaches the target score first wins.

Game Features

  • Play Rock, Paper or Scissors against the computer.
  • The computer picks Rock, Paper or Scissors at random each round.
  • Two match lengths: Best of 5 (first to 3 wins) or Best of 9 (first to 5 wins).
  • Live score for player wins, computer wins and ties. Ties do not count towards winning the match.
  • Clear win, lose and tie feedback showing both choices.
  • Unlockable Fire move: win a Best of 5 match to unlock it. Fire beats Paper and Scissors but loses to Rock.
  • Unlockable Dragon move: win a Best of 9 match to unlock it. Dragon beats every move but can only be used once per match.
  • Score, match length and unlocked moves are saved, so your progress is kept when you refresh or come back later.
  • Reset Game button to start a new match while keeping unlocked moves. Changing the match length also starts a new match.
  • Dark and light mode, remembered between visits.
  • Sound effects with an on/off control, remembered between visits.
  • Round results are announced to screen reader users.
  • Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile.

Our Hackathon Project

This project was developed collaboratively during our group hackathon. We worked together to plan the game, design the interface, develop the functionality and test the final product.

The project gave us an opportunity to practise HTML, CSS and JavaScript while working as a team and following user stories and acceptance criteria.
